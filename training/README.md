## Training

This training focuses on developing solid technical skills in Software-Defined Networking (SDN), with an emphasis on Open vSwitch (OVS) and Open Virtual Network (OVN). The objective is to enable students to design, implement, and operate virtual networks, as well as solve real-world problems in SDN environments.

The content is divided into progressive modules, each composed of theoretical fundamentals and practical exercises for reinforcement. To optimize learning, each module includes an automation script that provisions the necessary lab environment for performing the activities.
